Who We Are

KEEPING THE
TRADITIONS ALIVE

Three Aces Italian Canadian Cultural Club was founded in Toronto in 2013 by a group of friends who saw the traditions they grew up with starting to slip away. The Sunday lunches, the picnics, the whole family in one place. We started the club to keep the traditions alive, and to keep families and their children together the way our parents and grandparents kept us together.

You don’t have to be Italian. If you appreciate the Italian culture and way of life, the food, the family, the Sunday table, then everyone is welcome at ours. We're run by volunteers, and everything we raise goes back into the picnic, the food drive and the community.

Annual Picnic

THE 10TH ANNUAL
THREE ACES PICNIC

One Sunday every summer, our invite only picnic for the Three Aces famiglia and our supporters. Pasta, pizza, bocce, a raffle, a live performer, and the pool and splash pad for the kids, all made possible by our sponsors. Everything raised through clothing and the raffle goes straight back into the picnic and the food drive. All of it. Nine down, and the tenth is going to be a big one.
